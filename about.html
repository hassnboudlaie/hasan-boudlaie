About

Research and practice, connected.

Dr. Hasan Boudlaie is a management consultant, leadership advisor and executive educator with a PhD in Human Resource Management. He previously served as an Assistant Professor at the University of Tehran.

His work brings together organizational research and more than fifteen years of consulting and training. He focuses on strategy execution, organizational transformation, people and culture, and the development of management capability.
