A few things before you begin

This is not an assessment or an application. There are no right answers. Only six core questions are required; the remaining questions are optional. Please be candid, skip questions as needed, and focus on where you would value a mentor's perspective. Your priorities will guide our conversations.

Your answers are submitted privately to your mentor; they are not published on GitHub. Avoid confidential employer information, identifiable research participants, unpublished sensitive data, or details you do not wish to share. This is an independently prepared mentoring tool, not an official University of Birmingham questionnaire.
