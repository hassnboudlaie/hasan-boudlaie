# Specialized BMC — Content, Scientific Alignment & UI Audit
Date: 2026-11-06

## Scope
Only `business-model-canvas/parastoo-canvas.html` and `business-model-canvas/jewelry-canvas.html`, their shared specialized engine, styles, and industry question-bank data. The General BMC, child-cultural questionnaire, My Finance, and other apps remain out of scope.

## Provenance: important methodological clarification
The original 54 Parastoo and 45 jewelry questions were **custom-designed discovery prompts**, informed by the BMC model and the existing sites `ba-parastoo`, `zilux-jewels` and `rf-gold`. They were **not** copied from or psychometrically validated by a research study. The revision aligns the question domains with the references below. Industry reports and coaching standards inform topics, but do not validate any individual's untested business assumptions.

## Framework and content revisions

| Industry | Baseline questions | Revised questions | Key gaps addressed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Parastoo — personal brand, AI coaching, subscription | 54 | 60 | AI disclosure, consent, human escalation, testing/oversight, evidence of paid demand, high AI usage cost, per-subscriber contribution |
| Jewelry — luxury retail & digital boutique | 45 | 51 | Affordability shift toward lightweight products, self-purchase and gifting, coherent online–in-store journey, inventory integrity, gold-price scenario margins, lower unit-sales stress testing |

Both include all nine Strategyzer BMC blocks, 12 selectable risks, 12 selectable 90-day actions, free-text prompts, automatic browser-local saving, WhatsApp/share/download export and a per-block **Evidence Level** field.

### Decision-grade evidence taxonomy
- **Not yet checked** — insufficient evidence.
- **Assumption / estimate** — internal view, not independently established.
- **Customer/specialist interview** — qualitative evidence with named sample/date.
- **Observed behavior / test** — actual use or trial outcome.
- **Real sale / payment / contract** — transactional evidence.

Selection of a checkbox or completion of a question is **not** equivalent to validation. Review evidence quality, sample size, cost, regulatory requirements, and alternative explanations before making investment decisions.

## UI/UX remediation
- Separate brand-specific purple-rose and warm-champagne themes while reusing the same accessible, responsive form engine.
- Clear jump navigation, one BMC block visible at a time, priority markers for the first two questions in each block, and progress status.
- Clickable, live nine-block BMC visualization following the standard five-column canvas placement on desktop; compact two-column mobile layout.
- Per-block evidence-source controls; previous question indexes and localStorage keys retained.
- Expand/collapse detailed choices and free-text prompts; transparent research methodology section at the end.
- All content is editable locally; no automatic server submission or multi-device sync is claimed.

## Constraints and unverified assumptions
- Global trends cannot be assumed to have identical magnitudes in Kish or Iran; test locally.
- AI Coach readiness, paid subscribers, ROI, the licensing status for online jewelry retail, exact gold pricing rules, and client demand are not certified or proven by this questionnaire.
- Zilux and R.F Gold are **references within the same sector**, not assumed to share ownership or one corporate model.
- Uploaded user answers stay in that browser's localStorage until shared manually. Browser data deletion can erase answers; there is no automatic consultant inbox.
- The questionnaire provides a structured **discovery intake**, not an automatically verified final investment-ready business model.

## Follow-up recommendations
- Interview paying prospects and document evidence and dates; do not simply fill checkboxes.
- For Parastoo, test clear AI disclosure, usage-dependent cost, retention, and human handoff before scale.
- For Jewelry, verify actual inventory reconciliation, pricing/facture rules and allowed online sales workflow with local professionals before payment flows.
- For each model, derive one-page synthesis and test cards from the entered data **after** respondent shares the export.
