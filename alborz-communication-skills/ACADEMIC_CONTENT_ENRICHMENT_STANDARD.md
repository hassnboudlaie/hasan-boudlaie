## Locked product contract
**Do not alter:** course/session navigation; mobile-first visual identity; ten techniques in session 2; eight-stage sequence per technique; role-play, quiz, rubric, re-practice, WELCOME model. Source enrichment affects content, not page architecture.

## Main academic source
David A. Whetten and Kim S. Cameron, *Developing Management Skills*, 8th edition (2011).
Use as a **content source for skills**, NOT as an official insurance regulation, etiquette rulebook, or diagnostic instrument.

### Source-to-practice integration
- Ch. 4, pp. 233–278: supportive communication and listening (congruence, descriptive vs evaluative language, problem- vs person-oriented, validating, specific, conjunctive, owned communication). Adapt into live dialogues, correct/incorrect examples, feedback cues, listening questions, observer rubrics.
- Ch. 5, pp. 279–322: influence and assertiveness under unwanted pressure. Adapt into customer/manager requests, polite boundaries and realistic follow-through without promising unauthorized outcomes.
- Ch. 7, pp. 373–437: interpersonal conflict diagnosis, collaboration and conflict-handling approaches. Adapt into customer complaints, staff conflicts and multi-party simulations.
- Ch. 9, pp. 489–532: team membership, roles and giving feedback. Adapt to intra-branch leadership communication and respectful dialogue with staff.
- Supplement B, around pp. 624–628: elaboration, clarification and reflective probes; use for advanced listening and inquiry.
- Supplement C, pp. 651 onwards: Five Ps of Effective Meetings: Purpose, Participants, Planning, Participation, Perspective. Integrate into professional meeting scenarios, not as a new page.
- Across chapters: Skill Assessment → Skill Learning → Skill Analysis → Skill Practice → Skill Application; use as an *instructional design lens within the existing eight stages*, not a separate menu.

## Delivery standard for EVERY script turn in sessions 2 and beyond
A. Spoken text: natural Iranian workplace Persian, polite, credible, not stiff or legalistic; maintain insurance technical accuracy.
B. A separate **three-channel performance note appears BELOW each spoken turn**:
   1. **🎙 Voice and tone:** tempo, emphasis, pauses, volume and emotional register
   2. **🧍 Body language:** posture, position, gestures, gaze and distance; no unverifiable mind-reading
   3. **🙂 Facial expression:** contextually appropriate expression, not constant smiling, no definitive deception claims
C. Script should have challenging, differentiated roles; progressive resistance, appropriate concessions, questions, and a purposeful conclusion.
D. Keep original exercise framework: contextual scenario, techniques, three applied questions, two-person role-play, full annotated script, observer rubric, revised performance, and takeaway/action.
E. Include at least three instructor stop-points and prompts. Preserve instructor ability to toggle delivery cues and isolate each speaking role.
F. When a source-based behavior is introduced, identify chapter/reference in optional learning note, without over-claiming that the book prescribes industry-specific procedure.
G. The manager must NEVER guarantee insurance payouts, skip official approvals or disclose personal policy/customer information.

## Scientific and practical boundaries
- Do not generalize the 7–38–55 rule across communication, claim deception can be detected from facial expressions/body language, endorse hemispheric personality typology, or attribute disease deterministically to suppressed emotions.
- No one-size-fits-all protocols for clothing, handshakes or etiquette; comply with employer standards, culture, participant preference and local requirements.
- Avoid turning every question into a coaching session; the course emphasizes practice and performance.
- Write in professional conversational Persian, with English technical terminology only when useful; keep RTL readable.
- QA: content fidelity, realistic role dialogue, every utterance has voice/body/face, navigation unchanged, full-script filters, test feedback, mobile typography, all role links, teacher prompts.
