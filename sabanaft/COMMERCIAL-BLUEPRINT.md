# Reflective Leadership OS — Commercial Blueprint
Date: 2026-10-25. Status: product specification, not a launched subscription service.

## Promise and product architecture
A manager should enter a real leadership issue by typing or speaking, approve the transcription, receive context-sensitive clarifying questions and see defensible ways to act. The manager retains ownership of the decision. The application connects two distinct layers:
1. Persistent personal profile: leadership idea, four roles (Boss, Coach, Partner, Enabler), leadership context, responsibilities and dated snapshots.
2. Situation-specific case: event, decision, urgency, team capability, authority boundaries, context changes and real-world outcome.

Do not require the 18-dimension context map again for each case; ask only what changed. Never infer a deterministic right role from context scores.

## Five main journeys
- New Issue (Case-first homepage, optional text or voice)
- My Cases (Case Lab and roleplay)
- My Reflection (actual actions, evidence, next step)
- My Coach (future AI companion, human coaching or mentoring)
- My Impact (personal usage and follow-through metrics)

Profile, Context, Leadership Idea, backup and educational material belong in secondary navigation. Preserve existing tools and data.

## Voice and dialogue
A manager may optionally authorize microphone access to convert speech to editable text. The public pilot uses supported browser speech recognition; some browsers send audio to external recognition services. Explicit notice appears before recording. This is unsuitable for confidential company or personal data. Commercial voice processing requires a reviewed vendor, contract, data minimization, clear retention terms, deletion and secure handling of transcripts.
Roleplay in the public pilot has scripted, not AI-generated responses. The commercial capability can use a protected AI service for natural multi-turn persona simulation with after-action coaching reflection. A simulation is not a real employee's feedback.

## Subscriptions and coaching
Individual: own account, secure workspace, optional AI credits.
Organization: per-manager seats, invite and revoke, organization billing, privacy-preserving aggregate dashboard. Additional professional and enterprise packages may include SSO and coaching session credits.
Human service: 30- and 60-minute online/in-person coaching or mentoring with calendar booking. Case briefs are shared only with a specific professional after explicit choice by the manager.
No production billing, user authentication, AI Coach, secure multi-tenant storage or coach booking exists in the public GitHub Pages pilot.

## Measurement and renewal evidence
Do NOT equate activity count with leadership improvement. Define metrics with source, denominator, reporting window and uncertainty:
- Seat activation = managers completing a first useful case/reflection / invited seats.
- Meaningful engagement = activated managers doing >=2 useful actions during a defined 28-day window / activated managers.
- Decision documentation = cases with an explicit rationale / all cases in the period.
- Action transfer = cases with a concrete next step / all cases.
- Follow-through = due actions reviewed with recorded evidence / due actions.
- Voluntary perceived usefulness = respondent rating and response rate (self-report, not objective productivity).
- Business outcome = agreed operational indicator measured before and after the pilot, with an appropriate comparison group where feasible.
- Renewal assessment = meaningful adoption, user value, credible business outcomes, costs, and limitations.
- Monetary ROI only when both incremental attributable benefit and program cost can be defensibly measured. Otherwise report ROI as not established.

Private manager notes and coaching transcripts are never employer-readable by default. Enterprise analytics must aggregate with small-group suppression and strict server-side tenant authorization. Employee monitoring/privacy requirements require a lawful purpose and professional review.

## Delivery roadmap
1. Simplified UX and prototype case intake, speech, scripted roleplay, self-impact reports (in public pilot).
2. Security/identity and durable database, separate individual and organization entitlements, billing.
3. Secure speech services and AI Decision/Coaching engine.
4. Human booking, coach matching and session payments/credits.
5. Private corporate metrics pipeline, baseline pilot and renewal reporting.
